## Dimensions now available

[DB InfraGO's Ostkreuz station record](https://www.dbinfrago.com/web/bahnhoefe/leistungen/stationsnutzung/stationshalt/stationsausstattung/Berlin-Ostkreuz-12670236), dated 31 August 2026, gives these dimensions for the selected tracks:

| Tracks | Net built platform length | Platform height above rail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3–6 | 152 m each | 96 cm |
| 11/12 | 155 m each | 96 cm |

The app cites these when a platform is selected. Net built length is not operationally usable train length: DB distinguishes the two. Neither surveyed endpoints nor absolute elevation is established. Glyphs and vertical spacing remain illustrative. Equipment listings do not establish live availability.

The selected measurements were manually checked against the retained page. Source HTML stays in ignored `sources/engineering/`; the receipt records hashes and acquisition times. Only the small factual selection and citations are reproduced, without artwork or a blanket reuse licence claim. VBB and ATKIS retain their separate licences.

## Engineering cross-checks

[Schüßler-Plan's project account](https://www.schuessler-plan.de/de/projekte/s-bahnhof-ostkreuz-berlin.html) describes a 38 m wide upper island platform crossing the lower railway. It supports the structural arrangement, but supplies no surveyed platform endpoints or rail elevations. This width is not applied to individual track glyphs or used to reshape the ATKIS outline.

[KL's structural review account](https://kl-ing.de/projekte/projekt/umbau-s-bahnhof-ostkreuz-berlin/) describes the historical footbridge and platform E roof, built during 2013–2017. Its approximately 6.50 m measurement is roof ridge height above rail, not the separation between railway levels. It is excluded from scene spacing. Both pages are retained locally with receipts; their artwork is not used.

The search did not establish a current as-built rail elevation or approach profile. DB's [infrastructure register](https://www.dbinfrago.com/web/schienennetz/netzzugang-und-regulierung/infrastrukturregister) and [map access page](https://www.dbinfrago.com/web/schienennetz/netzzugang-und-regulierung/infrastrukturregister/interaktive_karte-11089022) remain candidate acquisition routes: no metric rail export or reuse contract was established in this pass.

## Inspection controls

The station has 1×–3× zoom and reset controls. Above 1×, selecting a platform centres the view on it; changing level separation keeps it centred. Footprint, tracks, connectors and moving marks share one display transform. Only viewing scale changes: source positions, dimensions and the shared timetable clock stay fixed.
